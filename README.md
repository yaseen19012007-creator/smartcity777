# 🏙️ NavaNagar – Smart City Website

### The City of Tomorrow | A Better City, A Brighter Future

## 📌 About the Project

NavaNagar is a Smart City website designed to showcase a modern, connected, cleaner, and more convenient urban environment.

The website provides information about essential city services, public facilities, transportation, healthcare, education, and important locations. Its main aim is to present how technology and digital services can improve everyday urban life.

## ✨ Features

- **Smart Transport:** Information about connected bus services and daily commuting.
- **Healthcare:** Information about hospitals, health centres, and emergency support.
- **Education:** Access to learning centres, libraries, and digital education services.
- **Public Utilities:** Information about water, electricity, and civic services.
- **Important Places:** Discover Central Green Park, City Civic Centre, and NavaNagar Transit Hub.
- **City Help Desk:** Contact information for project enquiries and assistance.

## 🛠️ Technologies Used
